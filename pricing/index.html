Questions

Straight answers

Does it work without internet?
Yes — that is the whole idea. Patients, refraction notes, recalls and every installed add-on live on your device, so the clinic runs with no connection at all. The internet is only needed for the optional daily cloud backup and for installing add-ons from the marketplace.
What happens to my data if I cancel?
Your records stay yours. Export everything whenever you like — patients, notes and recalls come out as plain files you keep. If you step down from a paid plan, your workspace keeps running on Solo, free, with the full offline EMR. Cancelling never locks or deletes your clinic records.
Do plans include the marketplace add-ons?
Solo comes with 12+ free add-ons — handy Extensions, capsules and building blocks. Practice and above unlock the entire catalog: every extension, capsule, theme and template, plus install codes and one-tap install from this site.
Is there a free trial for paid plans?
There is no trial to sign up for, because the free tier is not a demo — Solo is the full offline EMR, free forever. Run real clinics on it while you decide, then upgrade in the app when you want the catalog unlock, daily cloud backup or team features.
Can I pay yearly?
Yes. Practice is ₹1,999 a year — 2 months free compared to paying monthly. Clinic is ₹5,990 a year and Group is ₹14,990. One payment, once a year, nothing to track month to month.
What does "founding pricing" mean?
These are the early-adopter rates for practices in India. Join while founding pricing is on and your plan's price stays locked — later price changes don't reach the practices that came in early.
