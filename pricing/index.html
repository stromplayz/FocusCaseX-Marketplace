Questions

Straight answers

Does it work without internet?
Yes — that is the whole idea. Patients, refraction notes, recalls and every installed add-on live on your device, so the clinic runs with no connection at all. The internet is only needed for the optional daily cloud backup and for installing add-ons from the marketplace.
Is there a free plan?
No. Every plan is the full product — there is no feature-limited tier. Trials happen on your own machine before you buy: install FocusCaseX, load it with your own patients, and pay only when you're ready.
What's the difference between the plans?
Duration only. The product is identical on all three — same features, same marketplace catalog, nothing capped. What changes is how long the licence runs, and a longer run brings the effective monthly price down.
Do I pay for marketplace add-ons?
No — the whole marketplace is part of every plan. Extensions, capsules, themes, templates, install codes and one-tap install from this site are included whether you're on Monthly, Quarterly or Yearly. Nothing is gated by the duration you picked.
What happens to my data if I stop paying?
Your records stay yours. Export everything whenever you like — patients, notes and recalls come out as plain files you keep. Cancelling never locks or deletes your clinic records.
How do I pay?
In rupees — UPI, cards or net banking, taxes included. Install the app on your own machine first and try it on real work; then choose Monthly (₹399), Quarterly (₹1,299) or Yearly (₹4,499).
What does "founding pricing" mean?
These are the early-adopter rates for practices in India. Join while founding pricing is on and your plan's price stays locked — later price changes don't reach the practices that came in early.
