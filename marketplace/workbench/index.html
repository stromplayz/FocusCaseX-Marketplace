How the install works

Tap, hand off, confirm — the whole flow runs in the app.

Tap Install here

Your browser hands the WorkBench to the FocusCaseX app on this device — no copy-paste needed.

The app lists everything

The WorkBench opens inside the marketplace with its full contents — extensions, capsules, theme, templates, snippets and pages.

You confirm once

Tap Apply and the install runs in the app with per-step progress, then a final summary of everything that landed. Nothing is deleted; patient records are never touched.

Switch modes anytime

Applied WorkBenches appear as modes in the app — Marketplace → WorkBenches. Tap a chip to shuffle the whole workspace to another setup; your records stay put.

No app on this device? Type the code under Settings → Marketplace → Install by code, or see the install guide.

Build your own

Your setup, saved as one bundle.

Any clinic can turn its current workspace into a WorkBench. In the app, open Settings → Marketplace → Save current setup as WorkBench — FocusCaseX packs your extensions, capsules, theme, templates, snippets, pages and settings into one shareable bundle. Hand it to a second branch or a colleague and they install the whole setup in one step. The WorkBench Builder on the MCP & Automation page can also draft a starter bundle from a plain description of how your clinic works.
