FAQ — plain answers for clinics

The questions optometry clinics ask most, answered without jargon. The same list lives inline in the full guide.

The basics
Do I need the internet?
No. FocusCaseX works offline — your records live on your device and every board, page and note opens without a connection. The web app at focuscasex.vercel.app is there whenever you want it. The only online moment is the licence check when a device first activates.
What is FocusCaseX, in one sentence?
An offline-first clinic workspace for eye care: register patients, run refraction and exam flows, manage optics, dispensing and billing, set recalls — all on your own device, with pages you can read and print.
What is an Extension?
A small add-on that adds one feature to the workspace — an extra panel, an automation, a quick reference one tap away. You install it from the marketplace in one tap and remove it just as easily.
What is a WorkBench?
A whole clinic setup packed into one install — boards, pages, a theme, templates and starter settings. Applying one configures the workspace in a single step, and switching between installed WorkBenches works like switching modes. Your records never move.
What are capsules, themes, templates and snippets?
Capsules are ready-made boards you drop into any page. Themes change the look of every surface at once. Templates are pre-written clinical notes and letters. Snippets are small interface tweaks, like bigger headings.
How do I create my own Extension?
Follow the authoring guide — Create your own Extension: two small files, one catalog entry, a local test with the CLI, then a pull request to the marketplace. No build tools needed.
Licences and devices
How do I put FocusCaseX on another computer in my clinic?
Install the fcx command with one terminal line — the install guide has it — then run fcx setup and sign in with your licence email and the FCX-XXXXXX code issued for that machine. Every device counts against the licence's device limit.
What is the FCX code my clinic owner gave me, and where do I type it?
It is the activation code for your licence — eight characters, FCX-XXXXXX. In the app, a new device asks for it once at first launch, right after sign-in. On a computer, run fcx login in a terminal and answer the three prompts.
It says my code reached its device limit — what now?
Contact your clinic owner. They free a slot by revoking a device that is retired or lost, or raise the limit for your code — then the new device activates as normal.
I forgot my password — what now?
Ask your clinic owner — the password is reset on the licence. Nothing on your device changes, and your records stay exactly where they are.
Is my licence tied to one device? What is a trusted device?
The licence belongs to the clinic and carries a device limit — two devices by default. A trusted device is one that has activated once; it is remembered, and it never asks for a code again.
How do I remove FocusCaseX from a computer?
Run the installer with --uninstall — the command is removed. To free that machine's slot on the licence, ask your clinic owner to revoke it.
Can two clinics share one licence?
No. One licence is one clinic — codes are bound to a single email, and the devices under it share one workspace. A second clinic needs its own licence and its own email.
Your data
Does my patient data go anywhere?
No. Records live on your device. Activation and the periodic licence check send hardware identity only — never patient records. Export anything, any time, as plain files you keep.
Does installing an add-on touch my records?
Never. Installing or removing an add-on changes the setup only — your case files, documents and history stay exactly where they are.
What exactly works offline?
Everything you do day to day: patients, exams, notes, boards, recalls and every installed add-on. First activation on a new device needs a connection once; after that the device is trusted and the clinic runs without one.
Updates and sharing
How do I update the app?
The Android app checks for updates on its own and tells you inside the app when one is ready — you confirm and it updates. The web workspace is always the current build; there is nothing to do.
How do I update the fcx command?
Run fcx update — when a newer build ships, it prints the one-line install command to run. Re-running the original install command works too; fcx --version confirms the build afterwards.
Can I share the app with the front desk over the clinic network?
Yes. When your clinic has its own network, fcx serve hosts the marketplace for every computer on it — the command prints the address to open. No clinic network? Everyone uses the web workspace — it opens as usual from anywhere.
Contact
Who do I contact?
Your clinic owner for codes, device moves and password resets. For everything else, FocusCaseX support answers licence mail directly — the docs pages always point at the current contact.
